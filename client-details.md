## 6. E-Tuition — Coaching Academy Platform

**Live:** https://e-tution-platform.vercel.app/
**Sector:** EdTech · **Stack:** Next.js, Firebase/Firestore, Google Workspace APIs
**Role:** Full-Stack Developer & Product Owner

End-to-end operating system for coaching centres — replaces WhatsApp links,
shared drives, paper registers, and Word certificates with one workflow.

- Integrated Google Meet, Calendar, Drive, and Sign-in so classes are
  scheduled from the course and joined inside the platform, with meeting
  links exposed only to the teacher and enrolled students.
- Built automatic attendance capture at join time, and recordings that resume
  to the exact second across devices with per-lesson completion tracking.
- Shipped an AI notes pipeline (transcript, summary, key points, timecoded
  topics) gated behind mandatory teacher review before publishing.
- Implemented three strictly separated roles — administrator, teacher, student
  — enforced by Firestore security rules rather than hidden UI.
- Automated certificate issuance with serial numbers verifiable at a public
  URL, plus owner-facing reports recomputed from actual events.
